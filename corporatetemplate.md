## Purpose of this document

This document defines how content for the Corporate (B2B) vertical should be created.

The goal is not to show random events.

The goal is to make decision-makers (HRs, founders) feel:

* This is well-organized
* Employees will actually participate
* This is worth the investment

Every piece of content should move towards:

> Inquiry → Call → Booking

---

## Core Content Strategy (Corporate)

Your reality:

* You mainly sell **one-day events**
* Companies want **team outing + break from routine**
* Biggest doubt:

  > “Is this useful or just random games?”

So content must always answer:

* What is happening
* Why it matters
* What outcome it creates

---

## Content System Overview

We will use **5 structured content systems**.

Each one builds:

* Authority
* Clarity
* Trust

---

## 1. Event Story Breakdown (Your Most Important Format)

This replaces your current “random event montage”.

### Structure

Start with context:

“Last week, we conducted a team engagement session for [type of company]”

Then show:

* Team arrival
* Warm-up
* Games
* Participation
* Energy

But the difference is:

👉 You **explain while showing**

### Hook Styles

* “What actually happens in a corporate fitness event?”
* “Here’s how we engaged 40 employees in one session”
* “Not just another team outing…”

### Middle Layer (Critical)

Overlay or voiceover explaining:

* “Ice-breaker activities”
* “Team-based challenges”
* “Encourages participation”
* “Breaks routine work stress”

### Ending

* Group photo
* Smiling employees

### CTA

* “Looking to engage your team? Let’s connect”
* “Plan your next team session with us”

---

## 2. Problem → Corporate Solution

This is where decision-makers relate.

### Structure

Start with a real corporate problem:

* “Team engagement is low?”
* “Same routine, same meetings?”
* “Employees not participating in activities?”

Then build:

“Most activities fail because they are either boring or forced”

Then position:

“We design game-based fitness experiences that employees actually enjoy”

### Flow

Problem → Insight → Solution → Outcome → CTA

### CTA

* “Let’s plan something your team will actually enjoy”
* “Get in touch to design your session”

---

## 3. Structured Format Explanation (Build Authority)

This removes the biggest doubt:

> “Is this just random games?”

### Format

Coach / founder explaining the structure.

### Structure

Hook:

“Our corporate sessions are not random games”

Then explain:

* Warm-up phase
* Team grouping
* Game flow
* Engagement design

Keep it simple but structured.

### Tone

* Professional
* Confident
* Clear

### CTA

* “We customize based on your team size and goals”

---

## 4. Client Proof (Trust Builder)

This is critical for B2B.

### Format Options

#### A. Visual Proof

* Clips from multiple companies
* Logos (if allowed)
* Different team sizes

Text overlay:

* “20+ employees engaged”
* “High participation”
* “Team-based activities”

#### B. Testimonial

Client or HR saying:

* “Team really enjoyed”
* “Good engagement”
* “Different from usual activities”

### CTA

* “Trusted by teams across Chennai”
* “Let’s plan yours next”

---

## 5. Direct Lead Content (Decision Push)

This is where you convert.

### Format

Short, clear, no fluff.

### Hook

* “Planning your next team outing?”
* “Skip the usual activities”
* “Do something your team will actually enjoy”

### Body

* Show quick clips
* Add structured benefit

### CTA

* “DM or call to plan your session”
* “Limited slots available this month”

---

## Tone Execution (Very Important)

Your priority is:

> Professional + Authority

So:

* Keep language clean
* Avoid heavy slang
* Minimal memes
* Slight warmth is okay

It should feel like:

> A reliable service, not a random activity group

---

## Posting Guidelines

Corporate doesn’t need daily posting.

* 3–4 strong posts per week is enough
* Focus on clarity + proof

---

## What to STOP doing

* Random event videos with no explanation
* Only fun clips without structure
* No context posts
* Over-casual tone

If a corporate decision-maker sees your content and thinks:

> “This looks unstructured”

You lose the deal.

---

## CTA System

Rotate professionally:

* “Let’s plan your next team engagement”
* “Get in touch to customize your session”
* “Book a corporate fitness experience”
* “Connect with us for your next event”

---

## Final Clarity

Your biggest issue till now:

> Showing events without meaning

Your fix:

> Show structure + participation + outcome

Every post should answer:

* What is this?
* Why should a company do this?
* What will employees gain?

If those 3 are clear → you get leads.
