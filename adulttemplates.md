## Purpose of this document

This document defines how content for the Adult (B2C) page should be created consistently.

The goal is not to “post content daily.”
The goal is to create **repeatable formats that drive reach and trial bookings.**

Every piece of content must do at least one of the following:

* Stop the scroll
* Make people feel “this looks fun”
* Push them to try a session

---

## Core Content Strategy (Adult Page)

Right now, your strongest signals are clear:

* Montage edits + text → working
* Testimonials (ads) → bringing leads
* Static posts → not working
* Carousels → not working

So we **double down on what works**, not force what doesn’t.

This page will be:

> Reel-first. Experience-first. Energy-first.

Not:

* Design-heavy
* Information-heavy
* “Educating for no reason”

---

## Content System Overview

Instead of random ideas, we use **6 repeatable content systems**.

Each system has:

* A purpose
* A structure
* A hook style
* A CTA

---

## 1. High-Energy Game Montage (Core Growth Engine)

This is your most important format.

This is what builds:

* Reach
* First impression
* “This looks fun” feeling

### Structure

Start immediately with movement. No intro.

Show:

* Fast cuts of games
* People laughing
* Competition
* Group energy

Overlay text drives the narrative.

### Hook Style (Text on screen)

* “Gym pogama fit aagala nu feel panreengla?”
* “Work mudichitu tired ah irukeengla?”
* “Workout ah vida idhu fun ah irukum”
* “Ithu workout illa… game da”

The hook must:

* Call out a problem
* OR create curiosity
* OR break expectation

### Middle

* Show 3–5 different games
* Mix intensity + fun
* Show real people, not perfect bodies

### Ending

* Group shot / celebration
* High energy moment

### CTA

* “Free trial iruku. DM pannunga”
* “Try pannitu sollunga”
* “One session vandhu paarunga”

### Important Notes

Don’t:

* Add slow intros
* Add long explanations
* Over-edit

This format should feel:

> Raw + real + fun

## 2. Relatable Problem → Tribe Fortis Solution

This is where you connect emotionally.

Right now you are missing this layer.

### Structure

Start with a **very relatable problem**

Example:

“Gym join pannitu 10 days ku apram pogama vituteengla?”

Then shift:

“Problem neenga illa… system dhan”

Then show Tribe Fortis as solution:

* Games
* People
* Fun

### Flow

Problem → Frustration → Insight → Solution → CTA

### Hook Styles

* “Ellarum gym start pannuvaanga… but yarum continue panna maataanga”
* “Motivation irukum… aana 2 weeks dhan”
* “Workout boring nu feel aagudha?”

### CTA

* “Consistency venumna idha try pannunga”
* “Free trial open”

---

## 3. Testimonial Story (Conversion Engine)

You already know this works. But now we structure it.

### Format 1: Talking Head + Cutaways

Start with:

“Nan gym try panniruken… but continue panna mudila”

Cut to:

* Them playing
* Laughing
* Engaging

End with:

“Idhu dhan first time naan enjoy pannitu continue panren”

### Format 2: Text-led testimonial

Montage + text:

* “Before: Joined gym 3 times, quit”
* “After: Coming 4 days/week”
* “Reason: Not boring anymore”

### CTA

* “Same problem na DM pannunga”
* “Free trial available”

### Important

Keep it:

* Real
* Imperfect
* Not scripted

---

## 4. Coach Authority (Trust Builder)

Right now you are under-using coaches.

People need to feel:

> This is fun, but also legit.

### Format

Coach speaking directly:

But NOT boring fitness talk.

### Structure

Hook:
“Why most people fail in fitness…”

Then:
Explain in simple language:

* Boredom
* Lack of system

Then:
“Adhanala dhan we use games”

### Style

* Short
* Clear
* Slightly energetic

### CTA

* “One session try pannunga, puriyum”

---

## 5. First-Time Experience POV

This builds curiosity.

### Format

Show:

* New person entering
* Confusion
* Then playing
* Then enjoying

### Hook

“First time inga vandha feel idhu dhan”

or

“Neenga first time vandha ipdi dhan irukum”

### Flow

Entry → Hesitation → Playing → Smiling → Wanting more

### CTA

* “Neengalum try pannunga”

---

## 6. Direct Conversion Push (Aggressive CTA Content)

You said you’re okay being direct. Good. Use it.

### Format

Short, sharp, no fluff.

### Hook

“Still thinking ah?”

“Trial free nu sonnom la?”

“DM panna evlo kashtam?”

### Body

* Show gameplay quickly
* Add urgency

### CTA

* “Slots limited”
* “Book pannunga ippo”

---

## Posting Guidelines

Right now you are over-posting without structure.

Fix this:

* 1 strong reel per day is enough
* If quality drops, even 4–5 per week is fine

Priority:

> Quality hook > quantity posting

---

## What to STOP doing

* Single image posts
* Random carousels without strong hook
* Generic fitness education
* Over-designing content

If it doesn’t:

* Entertain
* Relate
* Or convert

Don’t post it.

---

## CTA System (Very Important)

Rotate these:

* “Free trial iruku”
* “DM pannunga”
* “One session try pannunga”
* “Experience pannitu decide pannunga”

Never end without CTA.

## Final Clarity

Every adult content should answer this silently:

> “Why will I show up here consistently?”

Answer should always be:

* It’s fun
* It’s social
* It doesn’t feel like workout

If your content doesn’t communicate this, it’s weak.
