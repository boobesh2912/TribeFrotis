## 1. Brand Overview

**Tribe Fortis is a Game-Based Fitness Company.**

We don’t run workouts.
We design **game-driven fitness experiences** that make people move, compete, and stay consistent without feeling like they’re working out.

We operate across 3 verticals:

* **Adults (B2C)** — Game-based fitness memberships
* **Kids (B2C)** — Athletic development through games
* **Corporate (B2B)** — Fitness experiences, events, and programs

---

## 2. Core Philosophy

Traditional fitness fails because:

* It’s boring
* It feels like work
* People lose motivation
* There’s no consistency system

**Our belief:**

> If fitness feels like play, people won’t quit.

We replace:

* Reps → Games
* Workouts → Challenges
* Gyms → Playgrounds
* Motivation → Systems + Community
